    • Interaction 2: Tilt to play a brass instrument

    Tap Turn On DSP and allow motion access. Hold your phone flat with its screen facing upwards, then tilt it left or right. Return it flat to stop blowing.

    We imagined a musical instrument controlled through movement. Greater sideways tilt increases the sound model's blowing pressure, allowing continuous control rather than a simple trigger.

    The first five degrees form a neutral zone to ignore small unintentional movements. Pressure reaches its maximum setting at about 45 degrees.

    Gesture mappings by Emilia Sandström and Liz Tulio.
    Based on the DT2140 starter project by Maurizio Berta and Laura McHugh.
