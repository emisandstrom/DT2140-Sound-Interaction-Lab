    • Interaction 1: Shake to ring a bell

    Tap Turn On DSP and allow motion access. Gently shake your phone to ring the bell. Keep silent mode off and use a comfortable volume.

    We imagined a virtual handheld bell. Shaking the phone resembles ringing a real bell, making the action and sound easy to associate.

    A 350 ms cooldown limits overlapping strikes. The shake threshold slider adjusts sensitivity.

    Gesture mappings by Emilia Sandström and Liz Tulio.
    Based on the DT2140 starter project by Maurizio Berta and Laura McHugh.
