    • Interaction 3: Large movements trigger thunder

    Turn on DSP and allow motion access. Hold the phone securely and make a quick, firm movement to trigger a thunder sound. Small movements should remain silent.

    Let the phone settle before repeating the gesture. You can also tap an empty area of the page to test the sound.

    We imagine a sound-effects controller for a performance. A strong gesture triggers a powerful thunder sound, while a movement threshold helps prevent accidental activation.

    Interaction mappings by Emilia Sandström and her laboratory partner.
